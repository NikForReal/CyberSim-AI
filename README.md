# 🛡️ CyberSim AI

> **A game-based cybersecurity simulation platform for practical security learning.**

CyberSim AI is an interactive cybersecurity training platform designed to teach cybersecurity concepts through realistic, simulated incident-response scenarios.

Instead of simply answering MCQs, players take the role of a **Cybersecurity / SOC Analyst**, investigate simulated incidents, analyze evidence, identify threats, apply defensive actions, and verify whether the incident has been contained successfully.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The goal of CyberSim AI is to make cybersecurity education more **interactive, practical, and engaging**.

Traditional cybersecurity learning often focuses heavily on theory and quizzes. CyberSim AI introduces a game-based approach where learners can:

- Investigate simulated security incidents
- Analyze digital evidence
- Identify attack patterns
- Apply simulated defensive measures
- Verify remediation
- Earn XP and rewards
- Progress through increasingly difficult missions

---
